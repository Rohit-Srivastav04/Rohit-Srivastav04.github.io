Privacy Policy

App: We Can Do Devil · Last updated: 12 November 2026 (AdMob-ready)

This Privacy Policy explains how We Can Do Devil (“the App”) handles information. The App is a single-player landscape platformer game.

1. Data we collect

The App does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos.

Progress data is stored only on your device using local storage, including:

  • level completion / unlock progress
  • death count
  • sound mute preference
  • theme preference (light / dark)

This data stays on your device unless you clear the App’s storage.

2. Internet / network

If you play the web / PWA version, standard web delivery may involve loading game files and fonts from hosting providers. The core game loop itself does not upload your gameplay progress to our servers.

3. Ads and analytics

The Android store version of the App may show ads served by Google AdMob (for example rewarded ads and interstitial ads). Google and its partners may collect device and advertising identifiers, IP address, and rough location / device data to provide, measure, and improve ads, subject to Google’s policies and the user’s device ad settings.

Learn more in Google’s advertising / privacy documentation for AdMob and in your device settings (for example, reset/delete advertising ID where available).

The web / PWA preview build may not show AdMob ads. Game progress remains stored locally on your device.

4. Children

The App is a casual skill/platformer game. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through a future update that collects data, contact us to request deletion.

5. Permissions

The Android package is intended to run as a standard game with no sensitive permissions (no SMS, call log, camera, microphone, or precise location required for core gameplay).

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Progress stays on your device. When ads are enabled, advertising partners such as Google AdMob may process device/ad data as needed to show ads. We do not operate a login account system that uploads your level progress to our own servers.

7. Security

Local device storage is used for preferences and progress. Protect your device with a lock screen and keep the App updated.

8. Your choices

  • Clear App storage / site data to reset progress and preferences.
  • Uninstall the App to remove local App data from your device.

9. Contact

For privacy questions about We Can Do Devil, contact the developer at the email listed on the Google Play store listing for this App.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when features change (for example, if cloud save, ads, or analytics are added). The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
